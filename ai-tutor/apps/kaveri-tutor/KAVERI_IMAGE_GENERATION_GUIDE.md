## General Image Guidelines for Gemini

### Style Requirements:
- **Art Style**: Colorful, vibrant, child-friendly illustrations (similar to Vaani app style)
- **Characters**: Diverse children, diverse skin tones, happy expressions
- **Color Palette**: Bright primaries (red, blue, yellow), pastels, natural tones
- **Cultural Elements**: Include Kannada script where visible (on signs, boards), Indian cultural items
- **Quality**: High resolution (800x600px minimum), clear lines, professional finish

### Composition Tips:
- Include Kannada text label in corner (small, readable)
- Center main subject
- Clear background (not cluttered)
- Emotions/actions clearly visible
- Age-appropriate content only

### Cultural Sensitivity:
- Respectful portrayal of all cultures
- Appropriate gender representation
- Include diverse family structures
- Avoid stereotypes
