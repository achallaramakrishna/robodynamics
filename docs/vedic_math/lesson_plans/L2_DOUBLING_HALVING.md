### Session Loop A-I

- A. Doubling — Multiply by 2:
  Coach opens with: Scene 1: Doubling. To multiply by 2, just add the number to itself. Split tens and units for speed.
  Board model: Show 2×37: split 30+7, double each: 60+14=74. Show the split method clearly.

- B. Multiply by 4 — Double Twice:
  Coach opens with: Scene 2: ×4 means double twice. First double, then double again.
  Board model: Show 4×23: 23→46→92. Draw two doubling arrows. Show 4×54: 54→108→216.
  Read aloud: Solve 4 × 23 by doubling twice. What do you get after the first double?
  Student try: Niagh, try this one. Say your first step, then your answer.

- C. Multiply by 8 — Double Three Times:
  Coach opens with: Scene 3: ×8 means double three times. Three arrows, three doublings.
  Board model: Show 8×11: 11→22→44→88. Show 8×25: 25→50→100→200.
  Read aloud: Solve 8 × 11. What is the number after the second doubling?
  Student try: Niagh, try this one. Say your first step, then your answer.

- D. Halving — Divide by 2:
  Coach opens with: Scene 4: Halving is the reverse of doubling. Split odd numbers as whole + remainder.
  Board model: Show 48÷2=24. Show 138÷2: 100÷2=50, 38÷2=19, total=69.
  Read aloud: Halve 138 using the split method. What is 100÷2?
  Student try: Niagh, try this one. Say your first step, then your answer.

- E. Divide by 4 — Halve Twice:
  Coach opens with: Scene 5: ÷4 means halve twice. Two arrows going the other way.
  Board model: Show 48÷4: 48→24→12. Show 92÷4: 92→46→23.
  Read aloud: Divide 48 by 4. After the first halving, what do you have?
  Student try: Niagh, try this one. Say your first step, then your answer.
  Reward and review: Correct. Divide by 4 — Halve Twice is now stronger and ready for the next step. Micro-practice: solve 64÷4 and 100÷4 by halving twice.
- F. Balancing Trick — One Doubles, One Halves:
  Coach opens with: Scene 6: The Balancing Trick. If one number doubles and the other halves, the product stays the same. This is the most powerful shortcut.
  Board model: Show 16×5: halve 16→8, double 5→10, so 8×10=80. Show scale balance visual.
  Read aloud: For 16×5, which number did we halve and which did we double?
  Student try: Niagh, try this one. Say your first step, then your answer.
  Reward and review: Correct. Balancing Trick — One Doubles, One Halves is now stronger and ready for the next step. Micro-practice: use balancing to solve 14×8.
- G. Multiply by 5 — Times 10 then Halve:
  Coach opens with: Scene 7: ×5 shortcut. Multiply by 10 first (add a zero), then halve. Super fast!
  Board model: Show 5×14: 14×10=140, 140÷2=70. Show 5×36: 360÷2=180.
  Read aloud: Solve 5×14. What do you get after multiplying by 10?
  Student try: Niagh, try this one. Say your first step, then your answer.
  Reward and review: Correct. Multiply by 5 — Times 10 then Halve is now stronger and ready for the next step. Micro-practice: solve 5×28 and 5×56 using the ×5 trick.
- H. Multiply by 25 — Times 100 then Quarter:
  Coach opens with: Scene 8: ×25 shortcut. Multiply by 100 first, then divide by 4 (halve twice).
  Board model: Show 25×12: 12×100=1200, 1200÷4=300. Show 25×36: 3600÷4=900.
  Read aloud: For 25×12, after multiplying by 100 you get 1200. Now halve it twice.
  Student try: Niagh, try this one. Say your first step, then your answer.
  Reward and review: Correct. Multiply by 25 — Times 100 then Quarter is now stronger and ready for the next step. Micro-practice: solve 25×8 and 25×20 using the ×100 ÷4 trick.
- I. Divide by 5 and 25 — Reverse Trick:
  Coach opens with: Scene 9: ÷5 and ÷25 reversal. ÷5 = double then divide by 10. ÷25 = ×4 then divide by 100.
  Board model: Show 70÷5: 70×2=140, 140÷10=14. Show 200÷25: 200×4=800, 800÷100=8.
  Read aloud: Divide 70 by 5. After doubling 70, what do you get?
  Student try: Niagh, try this one. Say your first step, then your answer.
  Reward and review: Correct. Divide by 5 and 25 — Reverse Trick is now stronger and ready for the next step. Micro-practice: solve 90÷5 and 300÷25 using the reverse trick.
