# Lesson 6: Number Splitting

## Snapshot

- Course: Vedic Math
- Lesson code: `L6_NIKHILAM_BASE_10_100`
- Duration: about 34 minutes
- Source: `Vedic Mathematics Manual | chap_6_number_spliting.pdf`

## Lesson Objective

- Split a long calculation into smaller mental chunks.
- Choose split points that reduce carries, borrowing, or awkward division.
- Use number splitting across addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.

## Teaching Approach

- A split is useful only when each chunk becomes easier than the whole problem.
- Read the chunk answers from left to right so place value stays correct.
- When a quotient chunk needs two places, keep the zero instead of dropping it.

## Duolingo Lesson Architecture

- Entry onboarding: ask name, comfortable language, current Vedic Math level, and session goal before starting the mission.

- Reward loop: Award 10 to 20 XP per exercise group based on the step position in the lesson path. Keep the streak visible on every step and protect it with immediate retries instead of long explanations.
- Review rule: Advance only after the learner can answer the checkpoint and complete one try in the same subtopic. Weak steps should return in the next session as review cards before new content.

### Session Loop A-I

- A. Addition by splitting into two easy chunks:
  Coach opens with: We split a long number into easy pieces, add each piece mentally, and read the answer from left to right.
  Board model: Show 2345 + 6738 as 23/45 + 67/38, then add 45 + 38 and 23 + 67.
  Read aloud: How does 2345 + 6738 become 9083 by splitting?
  Student try: Niagh, try this one. Say your first step, then your answer.
  Reward and review: Correct. Addition by splitting into two easy chunks is now stronger and ready for the next step. Solve 3456 + 4717 by splitting into two chunks and saying the right chunk first.
- B. Choosing the split line to avoid carries:
  Coach opens with: The best split line is the one that avoids carries crossing the line.
  Board model: Compare 481 + 363 with a split at 4/81 and 3/63, then show 4554 + 3636 as 45/54 and 36/36.
  Read aloud: Why is it helpful to choose a split where no carry crosses the line?
  Student try: Niagh, try this one. Say your first step, then your answer.
  Reward and review: Correct. Choosing the split line to avoid carries is now stronger and ready for the next step. Solve 4554 + 3636 and explain why the split after two digits works neatly.
- C. Subtraction by splitting:
  Coach opens with: Subtraction also becomes easier when we split both numbers in the same places and avoid borrowing across the split.
  Board model: Show 5454 - 1726 as 54/54 - 17/26, then compute 54 - 26 and 54 - 17.
  Read aloud: How does 5454 - 1726 become 3728 by splitting?
  Student try: Niagh, try this one. Say your first step, then your answer.
  Reward and review: Correct. Subtraction by splitting is now stronger and ready for the next step. Solve 852 - 139 by splitting into 8/52 and 1/39.
- D. Multiplication by splitting without carry:
  Coach opens with: For multiplication, split the number so each piece can be multiplied easily and without a carry.
  Board model: Show 352 × 2 as 35/2 × 2, then 604 × 7 as 6/04 × 7.
  Read aloud: Why do we split 604 as 6 and 04 before multiplying by 7?
  Student try: Niagh, try this one. Say your first step, then your answer.
  Reward and review: Correct. Multiplication by splitting without carry is now stronger and ready for the next step. Solve 814 × 4 by splitting it into two easy parts.
- E. Division by splitting into two sections:
  Coach opens with: Division can also be split when each section divides neatly by the same divisor.
  Board model: Show 432 ÷ 2 as 4/32 ÷ 2, then 3456 ÷ 2 as 34/56 ÷ 2, and 612 ÷ 6 as 6/12 ÷ 6.
  Read aloud: Why does 612 ÷ 6 become 1/02 instead of 1/2?
  Student try: Niagh, try this one. Say your first step, then your answer.
  Reward and review: Correct. Division by splitting into two sections is now stronger and ready for the next step. Solve 2849 ÷ 7 by splitting it into 28 and 49.
- F. Division with three sections and longer numbers:
  Coach opens with: Longer division can need three sections, but the idea is still the same: divide each section cleanly and keep the places.
  Board model: Show 24453 ÷ 3 as 24/45/3, then 391352 ÷ 13 as 39/13/52.
  Read aloud: How does 24453 ÷ 3 become 8151 by three-part splitting?
  Student try: Niagh, try this one. Say your first step, then your answer.
  Reward and review: Correct. Division with three sections and longer numbers is now stronger and ready for the next step. Solve 91827 ÷ 3 by splitting it into 91, 82, and 7.


## Worked Examples

- `2345 + 6738 = ?` -> Split as 23/45 and 67/38. Add the right chunk 45 + 38 = 83, then add the left chunk 23 + 67 = 90. Read the answer as 9083. -> answer: `9083`
- `5454 - 1726 = ?` -> Split as 54/54 and 17/26. Compute 54 - 26 = 28 and 54 - 17 = 37. Read the answer as 3728. -> answer: `3728`
- `604 * 7 = ?` -> Split as 6/04. Multiply each chunk by 7: 6 Ã— 7 = 42 and 04 Ã— 7 = 28. Read the answer as 4228. -> answer: `4228`
- `3456 / 2 = ?` -> Split as 34/56. Divide each part by 2: 34 Ã· 2 = 17 and 56 Ã· 2 = 28. Read the answer as 1728. -> answer: `1728`
